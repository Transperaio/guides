# Reference images for AI video generation: guiding the look of a video

A story concept tells an AI video generator what happens. Reference images tell it what things should look like. This guide helps marketing, training and publishing teams choose and prepare reference images before a text to video run, so the first result is closer to the look they have in mind.

## Before you start

- [ ] A written story concept, ideally a few clear scenes (see the concept, not the images, as the source of the story).
- [ ] The settings you need: duration, quality and screen ratio.
- [ ] Two to four candidate images that you have the right to use.
- [ ] A person who will review the finished video.

## Steps

1. **Decide what the images must control.** Pick one thing: a character, a product, a colour mood or a setting. Images that try to control everything pull in different directions.
2. **Choose images that agree with each other.** Keep a similar style, lighting and colour palette across the set. Expected result: one consistent look instead of a mix.
3. **Check each image for clutter.** Prefer a clear subject and a simple background. Remove images with text, logos you do not own or crowded scenes.
4. **Match the images to the screen ratio.** If the video is vertical, use images where the subject still reads in a vertical crop.
5. **Write the concept so it fits the images.** Name the character or object the way the images show it. Do not describe a look that contradicts them.
6. **Add the images as optional references and set duration, quality and screen ratio.** Generate the video.
7. **Compare the result with your references.** Note what matched and what drifted, then adjust the concept wording or swap one image and generate again.

## Common problems

- **The look is inconsistent between scenes.** Cause: references with different styles. Fix: replace the outlier and keep one style across the set.
- **A subject is missing or unclear.** Cause: the concept does not mention it or the image is cluttered. Fix: name the subject in the concept and use a cleaner image.
- **Unwanted details appear.** Cause: busy backgrounds in a reference. Fix: use images with a simple background.
- **The framing feels wrong.** Cause: images and screen ratio do not match. Fix: choose the ratio first, then pick images.

## Review

Watch the whole video before it is published. Check that characters and products look as intended, that nothing in the frame belongs to someone else, and that the images you supplied were yours to use. Generated video can differ from the references, so a person should approve it.
