# Editing a generated video by time reference: point to the moment, describe the change

This guide is for content and marketing teams who have a video from an AI video generation tool and want to change one part of it without rewriting the whole story. At the end you will have a short, reusable change request that names the moment in the video and says what should change there.

## Before you start

- [ ] You have a generated video you can play and scrub through.
- [ ] You have watched it once from start to finish and noted what needs to change.
- [ ] You know the original story concept, so a change does not contradict it.
- [ ] You know which part of the video each change belongs to (an approximate start and end is enough to begin).

## Steps

1. **Watch and mark.** Play the video and write down the time where the issue starts and where it ends. Example: "from 0:12 to 0:18".
2. **Pick one change per time range.** One range, one change. Do not mix "swap the background" and "shorten the greeting" in the same request.
3. **Describe the result, not the problem.** Write what you want to see or hear. Instead of "this part is wrong", write "show the product on a desk instead of in a hand".
4. **State what must stay the same.** Name the parts that are correct, for example the character, the voiceover or the other scenes, so the change stays narrow.
5. **Send the time reference with the description.** In Transpera Content Studio, editable time frames work this way: you give the time references (which part of the video) and the platform makes that change.
6. **Watch the edited range and the seconds around it.** Check that the edit joins smoothly with what comes before and after.
7. **Repeat for the next range.** Keep a short list of requests so you can see which ones are done.

### Example change requests (imagined scenario)

A marketing team has a product story video. They review it and write:

| Time range | Change request | Keep |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:05 | Open on the product name card instead of the street scene | Voiceover as is |
| 0:12 to 0:18 | Show the product on a desk instead of in a hand | Same character |
| 0:25 to end | End on the logo with a calm closing shot | Narration as is |

## Common problems

- **The change touches more than intended.** Cause: the time range is too wide or the request lists several changes. Fix: narrow the range and split the request.
- **The result differs from what you pictured.** Cause: the request describes the problem, not the outcome. Fix: describe what should be visible or audible.
- **A good part changed by accident.** Cause: nothing said what to keep. Fix: add a "keep" line for the character, voiceover or scene.
- **Time references do not match the moment.** Cause: the note was taken from a different version. Fix: replay the current version and note the times again.

## Review

- Play the whole edited video, not only the changed range.
- Check that characters, voiceover and look are still consistent across scenes.
- Have a person approve the final video before it is published. Generated video needs human review.
