# Schedule automatic posting for a generated video

This guide is for content and marketing teams that already use AI video generation and want the finished video to publish on a timetable instead of by hand. At the end you will have a repeatable routine: connect your social media platforms, create the video, set a schedule, and review before it goes out.

## Before you start

- [ ] A story concept for one video, written as a few clear scenes.
- [ ] Optional reference images that set the look.
- [ ] Decisions on duration, quality and screen ratio, based on where the video will be watched.
- [ ] Access rights to the social media accounts you want to connect.
- [ ] A named person who reviews the video before the scheduled time.

## Steps

1. **Write the story concept.** Keep to one idea per video. A text to video tool follows the concept closely, so vague wording gives vague results. Expected result: a short text with clear scenes.
2. **Add reference images if you have them.** Choose images that match the look you want. Expected result: the generated video follows the style of the images.
3. **Choose the settings.** Set duration, quality and screen ratio. A vertical ratio and a landscape ratio suit different places, so pick the one for the destination.
4. **Generate the video and watch it fully.** If a scene is wrong, refine the concept wording and generate again. Expected result: a video you would be willing to publish.
5. **Connect your social media platforms.** In the video pipeline, link the accounts that should receive the video. Expected result: the connected accounts are listed as destinations.
6. **Set the scheduler.** Choose when the video should be posted. Expected result: the generated video is posted automatically to the connected platforms at the time you set.
7. **Check the plan before the time arrives.** Confirm the destinations, the schedule and the video version that is queued.

## Common problems

- **The video does not fit the destination.** Cause: the screen ratio was chosen for another place. Fix: generate again with the right ratio.
- **The post goes to the wrong account.** Cause: an account was connected by mistake. Fix: review the connected platforms before you set the schedule.
- **The schedule runs before anyone has reviewed the video.** Cause: the review was left until the last minute. Fix: set the schedule later than your review deadline.
- **A video needs changes after scheduling.** Cause: a detail was missed. Fix: use time references to point at the moment and describe the change, then check the result again before the post time.
- **Viewers in other languages are not reached.** Cause: the video exists in one language only. Fix: dub the finished video into the target language, choose lip sync and hard or soft captions, and schedule that version too.

## Review

Automatic posting removes the manual step, not the need for judgement. Before the scheduled time a person should check:

- Spoken text, on-screen text and captions are correct.
- Characters, products and claims shown match what you can stand behind.
- Voices are used only with the owner's consent.
- Every connected account is one you intend to post to.
- Dubbed versions are checked by someone who knows the language.
