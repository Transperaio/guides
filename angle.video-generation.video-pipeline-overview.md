# AI video generation pipeline: generate a video, dub it, choose the captions

This guide walks through a three-stage video workflow: create a video from a written story concept (text to video), dub it into another language with lip sync, and decide how captions are shown. At the end you will have one finished video and a dubbed version with captions, ready for review.

## Before you start

- [ ] A short story concept, written as a few clear scenes.
- [ ] Optional: reference images that show the look you want.
- [ ] Decide the settings: duration, quality and screen ratio (where will the video be watched?).
- [ ] The target language for dubbing (the dubbing step supports 40 languages).
- [ ] A person who can review the result before publishing.

## Steps

1. **Write the story concept.** Keep one idea per video and describe it as a few scenes. Expected result: a concept text you can refine and run again.
2. **Add reference images (optional).** They guide the look of the generated video.
3. **Choose settings.** Pick duration, quality and screen ratio. A vertical ratio and a landscape ratio suit different places to watch.
4. **Generate the video.** An AI video generator turns the concept, images and settings into a video. Expected result: a first version to watch.
5. **Refine if needed.** Change the wording of the concept and generate again.
6. **Add the video for dubbing.** The same studio can dub the generated video, or any video you add.
7. **Pick the target language.** Choose from the supported languages: Indic (for example Hindi, Tamil, Bengali), European (for example Spanish, French, German) and others (for example Arabic, Japanese, Korean).
8. **Choose lip sync.** Lip sync aims to match the dubbed speech to the speaker on screen.
9. **Choose the captions.** Hard captions are burned into the picture and always visible. Soft captions can be switched on and off by the viewer.
10. **Run the dub and watch the result** from start to end.

Imagined scenario: a training team writes an onboarding video concept in English, adds reference images, generates the video, then dubs it into Hindi and Spanish with lip sync and soft captions.

## Common problems

- **The video does not match your idea.** The concept is too broad. Split it into one idea and clear scenes, then generate again.
- **The look is off.** Add or swap reference images that show the style you want.
- **Captions get in the way.** Hard captions cannot be removed later. Use soft captions if viewers should decide.
- **The picture is cropped on a platform.** The screen ratio did not fit the place where it is watched. Generate again with another ratio.
- **A dubbed phrase sounds wrong.** Machine dubbing can misread names, terms and idioms. Note the phrase and check it with a speaker of the language.

## Review

Machine-made video and dubbing need a human check before publishing. Watch the full video, listen to the dubbed audio with a speaker of the target language, check names and terms, read the captions for errors, and confirm the lip sync looks right on screen. Only voice cloning with the owner's consent.
